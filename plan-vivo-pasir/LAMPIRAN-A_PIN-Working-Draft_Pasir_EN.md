### 1.3 Land and Carbon Rights

[Draft wording — legal review required before use]
The Project Areas are located within state forest land for which the Minister of Environment and Forestry granted a Village Forest management approval (*Persetujuan Pengelolaan Hutan Desa*) to Pasir Village, managed by LPHD Pasir, through Decree SK.10885/MENLHK-PSKL/PKPS/PSL.0/2019 dated [TO BE CONFIRMED], for a period of [TO BE CONFIRMED — 35 years as per regulation, TO BE CONFIRMED in decree] covering [TO BE CONFIRMED] ha. Under this approval, LPHD Pasir holds the right to manage and utilise the Village Forest in accordance with its approved management plan. The land remains state forest land.

Under Indonesian law, carbon is governed by Presidential Regulation No. 110/2025 on Carbon Economic Value Instruments and National GHG Emission Control (which revoked Presidential Regulation No. 98/2021) and, for the forestry sector, Minister of Forestry Regulation No. 6/2026 on Procedures for Carbon Trading through GHG Emission Offsets in the Forestry Sector. Under Regulation No. 6/2026, holders of Social Forestry management approvals (including Village Forests) are eligible to trade carbon (Art. 6(1)(b)) with the support of a registered partner or facilitator (Art. 6(2)), which prepares the project design document for recording with the Ministry (Art. 13(1)(b)). Carbon units issued by an international standard require the Minister of Forestry's approval for issuance (Art. 10, 24), must be recorded in the national Carbon Unit Registry System (SRUK), and are subject to a non-tax state levy on transactions (Art. 46). Transfers used towards another country's NDC or other international obligations additionally require authorisation and a corresponding adjustment (Presidential Regulation 110/2025, Art. 68).
